## Watchlist

- OCC final rule under the GENIUS Act (by 2026-10-31): happened on 2026-10-21. News Release 2026-92 announces a final rule for permitted payment stablecoin issuers, foreign payment stablecoin issuers under OCC jurisdiction and certain custody activities of OCC-supervised entities. It takes effect with the GENIUS Act on January 18, 2027; BSA/AML and OFAC rules come in a separate rulemaking with Treasury, and the OCC will announce separately when it starts accepting applications and waiver requests. The release does not mention rewards paid through affiliates or third parties. [Regulator] [OCC NR 2026-92](https://occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-92.html) · 2026-10-21
  - Trade-press reading of the rewards question: the article says the rule keeps the ban on issuers paying yield directly to customers, removes a rebuttable presumption against stablecoin yield, and judges arrangements on their facts and circumstances; it reports that payments made solely for holding or using a stablecoin would likely stay out of bounds. Why it matters: this lowers the near-term risk to exchange-paid rewards such as Coinbase's USDC rewards, but case-by-case review keeps the named source of each payment material to C4 and L7. The rule text has not been read. [Press] [American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/occ-leaves-room-for-stablecoin-yield-maneuvers-in-final-rule) · 2026-10-21
