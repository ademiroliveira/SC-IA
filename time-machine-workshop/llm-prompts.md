# LLM prompts for the Time Machine workshop

Prompts for participants and the facilitator, for use after session 1. They come from the [facilitation guide](facilitation-guide.md), which says where each one fits.

Use an LLM after people write, never before. A model's answer is the average future, the very thing the guess slides teach the room to distrust. Ask it to question and argue, not to invent milestones. Stickies may touch unreleased plans, so use only an LLM your company approves.

| Prompt | Who | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Sharpen a milestone | Any participant | Between sessions 1 and 2, optional |
| 2. Stress-test a theme | Participants, about two per theme | Between sessions 1 and 2 |
| 3. Check the clusters and draft the recap | Facilitator | Within a day of session 1 |
| 4. Build the signal pack | Facilitator | Before sending the themes |

## 1. Sharpen a milestone

```
Help me sharpen one milestone from a futures workshop on crypto wallets. Don't add new ideas or change what I mean.

Context: a milestone is a shift in what wallets are or do, between 2027 and 2040, not a feature. The history so far, as problems that moved: don't lose the file → write down 12 words → understand what you're signing → decide what this account, app or agent may do. Open problems today: recovery, signing comprehension, supply chain, delegation sprawl, cross-chain complexity.

My sticky: [text · year · why us · Lead, Join or Watch]

1. Ask me up to three questions that would make it more specific, then wait for my answers.
2. Rewrite it as "By [year], [who] can [do what] without [what they do today]", keeping my words where you can.
3. In one line each: is it a shift or a feature? Is the year when it becomes normal or when it's first shown? Does the tag hold: if we stopped, would it still happen?
```

## 2. Stress-test a theme

```
Our team wants to [lead / join / watch] this milestone theme: [theme name, year, and the stickies in it]. Be a skeptical colleague, not a cheerleader.

1. The three strongest reasons it won't be normal by [year].
2. The strongest reason it shouldn't be us.
3. Up to four things that would have to be true by 2030 for it to happen.
4. Two early signs we could watch for in the next 12 months.

Say when you're unsure. Don't invent statistics, products or dates; give a source for anything you cite.
```

Bring the strongest objection to the session 2 recap. Keep answers 3 and 4: they feed the backcast and the pre-mortem in session 3.

## 3. Check the clusters and draft the recap

```
Below are the milestone stickies from session 1 of our wallet futures workshop, transcribed one per line: text · year · tag (L, J or W) · votes · the cluster name the room gave it.

[paste]

1. Flag stickies that read as features rather than shifts, and any that sit in the wrong cluster.
2. Note imbalances: crowded or empty lanes, years nobody chose, open problems nobody touched.
3. Draft the recap: the top three or four themes by votes, each named "From ___ to ___", with one tag and one year.

Use only what is in the stickies. Don't add milestones of your own.
```

The room's own clusters stand. Use this to check them and to save time on the recap, not to re-cluster.

## 4. Build the signal pack

```
Our milestone themes, each with its tag and year: [paste]

This week's signal report: [paste]

For each theme, pick up to two signals that support it and up to two that cut against it. One line each: what happened, the date, the source link, and why it matters for the theme. Use only signals from the report. If a theme has nothing relevant, say so.
```

The signals that cut against a theme open the session 2 recap.
