Wallet Time Machine · Session 1

03 / 31

Walk the milestones in about a minute. A milestone is a shift in what wallets are or do, and that is the same thing we will ask people to write later. The point is rhythm: a major shift every two to four years. The early years were about keys: where they live and how to back them up. Since 2023 the pace has picked up: smart accounts, upgrade-in-place accounts and agent wallets within three years. End on the last cell, 20??: the next one is ours to write. Leave it hanging; the question comes later. Hacks and collapses are deliberately not here; they come next, on their own slide.
