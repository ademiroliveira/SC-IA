This is the session’s decision, so let the question fill the screen and read it out loud: which would we regret not driving? Without it, some people vote for what is likely and others for what is exciting, and the result mixes the two. Check first that each cluster is named From ___ to ___. Vote in silence before any discussion, three dots each, stacking allowed. Name the top three or four clusters as themes, and keep each one’s Lead, Join or Watch tag and its year. The top one goes into the story on the next slide; all of them open session 2.
