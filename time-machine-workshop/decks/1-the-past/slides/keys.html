BIP32 and BIP39 (2012–13) turned one seed into a backup for every account, which made wallets portable and created the seed phrase as a bearer instrument and a phishing target. Trezor shipped in 2014 and the device screen became the security boundary. Mt. Gox collapsed the same year.
