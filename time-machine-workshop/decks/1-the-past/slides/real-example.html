This one happened, so it shows a whole sticky without seeding a future idea for people to copy. Read it as if it were 2012: the sentence, why us and the tag. Then the hindsight. BIP32 (2012) and BIP39 (2013) made one backup restore every account, and wallets adopted it. It passed all three tests, and it still moved the problem: the 12 words became a bearer instrument and a phishing target. Expect the same of ours: a milestone moves the problem, it doesn’t end it.
