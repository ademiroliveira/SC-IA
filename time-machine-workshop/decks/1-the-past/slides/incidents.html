Wallet Time Machine · Session 1 · Not milestones

04 / 31

Keep these apart from the milestones on the last slide. A hack or a collapse is an event; the milestone is the shift it pushes. Mt. Gox and FTX pushed people toward holding their own keys. Bybit showed that the signing interface, not the key, can be the weakest link. The newest is Binance on October 29, 2026: by TRM Labs’ count about $6.3B drained from hot wallets on ten chains, the largest crypto theft on record, which TRM attributes to North Korea’s TraderTraitor unit. On Ethereum it ran through EIP-7702, one of the milestones on the last slide, used against its owner. Binance said it had covered 90% of the stolen amount from its own funds. The pattern across Bybit and Binance: the signing system keeps being the target. If someone later writes an incident on a milestone sticky, ask what shift it would push, and write that instead.
