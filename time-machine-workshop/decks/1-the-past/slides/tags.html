Driving a milestone and being part of one are different bets. Lead means we make it happen; Join means we ride a shift others drive, such as clear signing or a new account standard; Watch means it matters but is not ours to move. Ask people not to tag everything Lead. The tags come back in session 3, when we sort moves into no-regret, bet and hedge.
