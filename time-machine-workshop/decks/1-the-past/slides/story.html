Wallet Time Machine · Session 1

Now pull the four eras into one line. Each shift moved the main design problem, not the technology: first the user had to keep a file, then twelve words, then understand every signature, and now decide what accounts, apps and agents may do. Then ask who in 2012 would have predicted the last two lines. Expect almost none: almost nobody in 2012 predicted passkeys, smart accounts or agent wallets. Say: ours will be wrong too; the value is in the reasoning. That sets the tone for writing boldly later. Finally, pause on the last row and ask: so what is the next line? Don’t take answers yet. The open problems come next, then the question, and we come back to this slide at the end of the session.
