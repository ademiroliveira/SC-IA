Eight minutes in silence, before anyone speaks, so the loudest idea does not set the agenda. Call the timing out loud: one minute to read, four to write as many as they can, two to rewrite the best two or three in the frame, one to add why us and a tag. Leave the starters on screen. Keep laptops and chatbots closed for now: a model’s answer is the average future, the very thing the guesses taught us to distrust. The LLM prompts come after the session.
