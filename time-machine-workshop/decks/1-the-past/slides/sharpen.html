Wallet Time Machine · Session 1 · Same as the card in your hand

25 / 30

Three minutes, back in silence. Each person picks their best two or three, rewrites them in the frame, and runs the three tests on each: is it a shift or a feature, is the year when it becomes normal, and would it happen without us? Then a tag and one line on why us. The third test matters most: most rooms tag too much as Lead. This is the same as the printed card, so people can work from either.
