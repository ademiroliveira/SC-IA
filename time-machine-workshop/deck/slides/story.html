The story in one line

Each shift moved the main design problem

2009–2013

Don’t lose the file

Keys sit in wallet.dat. The user is the backup.

2013–2016

Write down 12 words

One seed backs up everything, and attracts phishing.

2016–2024

Understand what you are signing

The wallet approves actions for every app.

2024–today

Decide what this account, app or agent may do

Permissions and recovery become design material.

Still open today: recovery without a seed phrase.
