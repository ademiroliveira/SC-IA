The exercise

Two sessions, 60 minutes each

Session 1 · Forward jump

Build two opposing futures

Two teams of four fill a world strip, 2040 first, then 2035 and 2030. Each team pitches its world to the other.

Session 2 · Return trip

Walk back to today

Teams swap worlds and ask what had to be true at each step, then vote on what to ship, test or decide next.
