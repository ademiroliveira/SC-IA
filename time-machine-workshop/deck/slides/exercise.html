Session 1 · Forward jump

Walk back to today

From 2040 to moves worth making now.

Wallet Time Machine

18 / 28

Session 1 is the forward jump: two teams of four each build a three-column world strip starting at 2040, then pitch their world to the other team. Session 2 is the return trip: teams swap worlds, ask what had to be true at each step, and vote on what to ship, test or decide in the next 12 months.
