11 / 28

ERC-4337 made smart accounts possible without a hard fork, with sponsored gas and scoped session keys. EIP-7702, live since May 2025, lets existing addresses gain batching, sponsored gas and scoped permissions without moving funds. Passkeys and embedded wallets made seedless onboarding a commodity, and the vendors consolidated: Stripe bought Privy and Fireblocks bought Dynamic.
