# Recovery and custody

**Runs:** monthly only. Weekly recovery news comes from the signal report.

**Covers:** claims C2, C7 and C8 and lenses L2, L3 and L4, across the market.
- **Recovery flows** of the scored competitors (Robinhood, MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Kraken, Phantom): what happens when a phone is lost, what the provider can and cannot do.
- **Investor-grade custody and inheritance:** Casa, Bitkey, Unchained, Nunchuk, Ledger Recover and new entrants; prices and changes.
- **Smart-account and guardian models:** Safe, Ready, Zengo and passkey wallets; passkey sync risks.
- **Rehearsal:** does any product rehearse a restore with the user, or verify that a backup works? This is a core bet; report honestly when nothing is found.
- **Friction:** time locks, cooling-off delays, withdrawal delays, allowlists, duress features, spending limits, one-tap revoke.
- **Incidents and data:** wallet compromises, coercion attacks, recovery failures and lost-access data published inside the window.

**Search terms:** wallet recovery, lost phone, passkey recovery, guardian, inheritance, key check, health check, time lock, withdrawal delay, spending limit, revoke approvals, wrench attack, plus the month and year.
