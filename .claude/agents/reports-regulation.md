# Reports and regulation

**Runs:** monthly only. Weekly regulatory news comes from the signal report and the watchlist.

**Covers:** facts and published analysis, never legal advice.
- **Regulation:** the GENIUS Act rules (OCC, Fed, Treasury) and how platforms route stablecoin rewards; the CLARITY Act; SEC actions on non-custodial interfaces, curated vaults, adviser status, custody and AI agents; CFTC; IRS 1099-DA and cost basis; state actions that explain competitors' state exclusions.
- **Industry reports:** ownership and allocation surveys, allocation guidance from asset managers and brokerages, self-custody adoption, curated vault and onchain earn data, tokenized treasuries reaching retail.
- For each item, say which competitors it affects and which claims or lenses it touches.

**Start here:** read this month's weekly signal reports first (you receive them as already covered), then the Regulation and reports table in `watch/sources.md`.

**Search terms:** GENIUS Act final rule, stablecoin yield rewards, CLARITY Act, SEC non-custodial interface, crypto custody rule, DeFi vault adviser, 1099-DA cost basis, crypto ownership survey, crypto allocation guidance, plus the month and year.

**Hand off:** product launches → `direct-threats` or `wallet-challengers`.
