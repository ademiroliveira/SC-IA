# Wallet challengers

**Runs:** weekly and monthly.

**Covers:**
- **MetaMask:** Money Account (rate, fees, protocols, curator, regions including the US), Agent Wallet (limits, general availability, revoke), Transaction Shield and protection.
- **Automated portfolios in self-custody:** Glider, Bitwise Automated Token Portfolios, Parrot; US availability above all.
- **Other wallets:** Phantom (CASH yield), Trust Wallet Earn, Uniswap Earn, Ledger Wallet, Tether's wallet, Exodus, Jupiter, Zerion, Coinbase Wallet's investing features.
- **New entrants:** any self-custody product launched or announced inside the window with earn, allocation, rebalancing, baskets, guardrails or AI-agent features, especially in the US.

**Watch especially:** any self-custody product that adds target allocation with drift or rebalancing, or user-defined rules enforced onchain. That is the gap the positioning claims (C1 with C2, and C5′).

**Search terms:** self-custody wallet plus earn, yield, vault, automated portfolio, rebalancing, allocation, basket, smart account, session keys, agent wallet, spending limits, plus the month and year.

**Start here:** the Wallets and new entrants table in `watch/sources.md`.

**Hand off:** Robinhood, Coinbase's exchange products and Kraken DeFi Earn → `direct-threats`; recovery flows → `recovery-custody`; fees and eligibility tables → `pricing-naming`.
