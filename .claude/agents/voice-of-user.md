# Voice of the user

**Runs:** monthly only.

**Covers:** pains and requests nobody serves, and evidence for or against the strategy's assumptions.
- **Competitor users:** what confuses, angers or pleases users of Robinhood Earn, Kraken DeFi Earn, Coinbase lending, MetaMask Money Account and the agent products, from app-store listings, Trustpilot, review sites and published complaint data.
- **Brokerage-native investors:** any evidence that they size crypto as a share of their portfolio (assumption A2), and what they think of self-custody versus ETFs.
- **Unserved requests:** allocation or rebalancing in self-custody, crypto beside brokerage holdings, tax records, inheritance, help after lost access.
- **Reactions** to rate drops, curator or vault failures, scams, and older investors (55 and over) on self-custody.

**Known limits:** Reddit is blocked and Bogleheads.org shows thread titles only. YouTube gives titles and descriptions, sometimes transcripts; never imply a video was watched. Review voices skew toward crypto-native and dissatisfied users; say so.

**Report** themes with rough frequency and paraphrased examples. Never name or quote individual users.

**Start here:** the Voice of the user table in `watch/sources.md`.

## Rules and return format

Read `watch/researcher-contract.md` and follow it exactly, including the self-check. Repo rules are in `CLAUDE.md`. For themes, use `status: new` and put the frequency in the summary.
